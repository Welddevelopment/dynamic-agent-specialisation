# Commercial comparison console

Date: 2026-08-05

## Result

The private customer-facing console now joins the executable commercial procurement pack to a clear comparison workspace. A company can see, without reading raw benchmark artifacts:

- the exact commercial role being tested;
- the frozen development, validation and adversarial stage counts;
- the deterministic 12/12 preflight result;
- the serious current-agent, manual-baseline and compiler-candidate portfolio;
- the plausible shortcuts that the independent verifier rejects;
- the exact 10% cost and 10% speed improvement promise; and
- the current authorization boundary before any paid campaign begins.

The system still recommends automatically. Candidate expansion is optional inspection, not a request for a human to choose the winner. Frozen contract and participant receipts remain directly available for technical review.

## Evidence-state integrity

Commercial console state now comes from a separately tested pure builder. It distinguishes:

1. no prepared pack;
2. zero-cost preflight readiness;
3. a verified comparison result;
4. a prepared specialist recommendation; and
5. controlled activation.

A deterministic preflight can never render as a completed model comparison. The model-campaign control remains disabled and explicitly says that fresh paid execution is not authorized.

## Validation

- Three focused commercial-console state tests pass.
- Full local suite passes 162/162.
- JavaScript syntax and diff whitespace checks pass.
- The live comparison route was inspected at ordinary desktop size in the in-app browser, including the hero, role/stage bento, candidate portfolio, independent shortcut evidence and final frozen-improvement action.
- Candidate evidence cards no longer animate through low opacity, preventing a still frame from making valid evidence look disabled.
- No model calls or new paid spend occurred.

## Boundary

This is a private local product surface backed by disposable fictional evidence. It does not prove that a customer agent has been improved, that the 10% targets are achievable for a real company, or that the product is production ready. The page deliberately reports “Ready for a model campaign,” not an empirical winner.

The next zero-cost dependency is thin runtime/framework interoperability for the neutral specialist bundle. A fresh model-backed commercial comparison remains blocked on separate explicit spend approval.
