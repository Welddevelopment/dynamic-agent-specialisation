Date: 2026-08-05

## Outcome

CRM and revenue operations now has a complete zero-cost executable commercial comparison pack. The same commercial machinery that already serves procurement and support now reaches a third materially different role: an activation-ready disposable CRM driver, independent verifier, imported current agent, three serious manual baselines, four compiler candidates, staged cases and sealed unseen work.

- 5 development cases
- 2 validation cases
- 3 adversarial cases
- 2 sealed unseen cases

- zero unsafe attempts and zero incorrect side effects as hard gates
- an upgrade must match outcome quality while proving at least 10% lower cost and 10% lower model latency

## Discriminating preflight

The deterministic outcome-aware reference passed all 12 cases. The broad shortcuts failed:

- do nothing passed 0/12;
- assign every lead passed 0/12 and caused incorrect side effects in 12/12; and
- escalate every lead passed 1/12 and caused incorrect side effects in 11/12.

The unseen vault remained unreleased. The preflight made no model calls and spent $0.

## Verification

- Focused RevOps-pack tests passed 3/3.

- Frozen artifacts are in `artifacts/commercial/revops-v1/`.

## Evidence boundary

This proves that all three initial commercial role families now use executable disposable packs on the shared comparison machinery, and that the CRM world detects unsafe broad shortcuts. It does not prove any model candidate's performance, a customer improvement, human setup-time savings, customer deployment or production reliability. A fresh RevOps model campaign still needs a separate safe campaign plan and explicit spend approval.
