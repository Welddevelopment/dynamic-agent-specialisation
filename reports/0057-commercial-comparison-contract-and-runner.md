# Commercial comparison contract and runner

Date: 2026-08-04

## Outcome

The commercial onboarding record can now enter a separate fail-closed comparison boundary. This prevents a smooth customer experience from weakening the Level 1 evidence design.

## Frozen before execution

The contract binds:

- the normalized company intake and compiled role draft;
- one verified role driver in a disposable sandbox, customer-local test environment or explicitly approved live boundary;
- a verified adapter for every named company system;
- one independent verifier;
- the current agent when supplied;
- strong-general, ordinary-manual and expert-manual baselines;
- the complete compiler candidate portfolio and maximum candidate count;
- development, validation and adversarial payloads;
- hashes for sealed unseen payloads;
- quality, cost and speed priorities;
- minimum improvement promised to the customer;
- zero unsafe and zero incorrect-side-effect gates;
- minimum fresh repeat runs; and
- hard model-spend and elapsed-time ceilings.

Mutation after commitment invalidates the freeze.

## Joined runner

The generic runner executes participant implementations through an injected role evaluator. Every observation must identify the exact frozen independent verifier and carry a verification receipt. It records outcome, safety, incorrect effects, model cost, elapsed time and human intervention.

The order is:

1. development;
2. validation;
3. adversarial;
4. sealed unseen release;
5. frozen unseen comparison;
6. selected-specialist fresh repeatability; and
7. activate, retain existing, or select another proven baseline.

An unsafe or incorrect candidate is eliminated before unseen evaluation. The runner refuses to release unseen cases if no compiler candidate clears the pre-unseen gates. A projected call outside the frozen budget is refused before evaluation. Actual spend is checked again after every observation.

If the compiler candidate does not beat the supplied current agent by the frozen improvement threshold, the result is `retain-existing-unproved-upgrade` even when the compiler candidate is slightly faster or cheaper.

## Validation

New tests verify:

- full freeze construction and hidden unseen payloads;
- system-adapter coverage;
- mandatory baseline and current-agent inclusion;
- bounded candidate count;
- gated unseen release;
- mutation detection;
- honest retention of an existing agent when improvement is unproved;
- unsafe-candidate elimination before unseen release; and
- budget refusal before an over-limit projected call.

Complete local suite: 150/150 passed. No paid model call was made.

## Remaining boundary

This checkpoint proves orchestration and fail-closed evaluation control. The role evaluator used by a real company must still be supplied through an executable customer adapter and external verifier. A fresh model-backed support comparison through this new generic runner has not run. Customer value, integration ease, human-effort reduction and production reliability remain unproved.
