## Executive result

Piece 2 is complete.

The compiler generated multiple complete procurement specialists, ran cheap viability screening, completed a four-case development tournament, eliminated weak candidates, attempted controlled refinement, survived two honest failed adversarial cycles, compared Luna and Terra variants, passed fresh validation and adversarial suites, competed against serious model and deterministic baselines, froze the full evaluation, passed both unseen cases, and repeated those cases three additional times without configuration changes.

The final compiler-generated Luna specialist achieved:

- 10/10 exposed regression cases before final testing;
- 5/5 fresh Cycle 3 validation/adversarial cases;
- 2/2 frozen unseen cases;
- 6/6 repeated formerly unseen outcomes;
- zero denied actions in those final stages;
- successful exact no-op completion, authority handoff, unapproved-supplier rejection and lost-response reconciliation.

This establishes that the compiler can create a genuinely functional, safe and repeatable specialist for one bounded fictional procurement role.

## What Piece 2 proves

- A model-backed compiler can construct valid complete specialist configurations.
- A generated specialist can safely operate a substantial fictional procurement environment.
- Automatic failure-grounded refinement can correct real scope and no-op behaviour across versioned evaluation cycles.
- Automatic model selection can reject a roughly ten-times-more-expensive model when it provides no accuracy gain.
- The final generated specialist generalized to two frozen unseen cases and repeated consistently in this bounded role.
- The evaluation layer can correctly recommend no upgrade when an existing setup remains marginally preferable under declared priorities.

## What remains unproved

- The generated specialist does not beat the ordinary manual baseline on accuracy or the frozen ranking.
- Human configuration-time savings were not observed prospectively; only baseline setup protocols were predeclared.
- Two unseen cases are too few for a strong reliability estimate.
- This is one fictional procurement role, not three substantially different roles.
- The complete bounded Level 1 finish line is therefore not met.
- There is no customer deployment, willingness-to-pay evidence, production reliability, revenue or funding evidence.
- No broad superiority claim over LangChain, CrewAI, Microsoft, OpenAI optimisation tooling or other systems is supported.
