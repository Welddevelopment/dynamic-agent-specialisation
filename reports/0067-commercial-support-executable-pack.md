# Commercial support executable pack

Date: 2026-08-05

## Outcome

The first polished commercial role is no longer onboarding-only. Customer support operations now has the same complete zero-cost executable comparison foundation as procurement: an activation-ready disposable driver, independent verifier, imported current agent, three serious manual baselines, four compiler candidates, staged cases and sealed unseen work.

## Frozen comparison world

- 5 development cases
- 2 validation cases
- 3 adversarial cases
- 2 sealed unseen cases
- 8 participants: current agent, strong-general, ordinary-manual, expert-manual and four compiler candidates
- exact support tool namespace covering tickets, accounts, billing events, incidents, knowledge, policy, responses, bounded credits, escalation, incident linking, duplicate merging and closure
- independent verifier checking per-ticket outcome, correct completion/handoff, authority, duplicate safety, assigned-queue scope and protected-state preservation
- zero unsafe attempts and zero incorrect side effects as hard gates
- an upgrade must match outcome quality while proving at least 10% lower cost and 10% lower model latency
- at least three fresh repeat runs before selection can become a specialist bundle

## Discriminating preflight

The deterministic outcome-aware reference passed all 12 cases. Three plausible but wrong broad strategies each passed 0/12:

- do nothing;
- close every open ticket; and
- escalate every ticket to product engineering.

The unseen vault remained unreleased. The preflight made no model calls and spent $0.

## Verification

- Focused support-pack tests passed 3/3.
- The complete local suite passed 192/192.
- Frozen artifacts are in `artifacts/commercial/support-v1/`.

## Evidence boundary

This proves that the shared commercial comparison machinery is executable across a second materially different role family and that the support world rejects broad shortcuts. It does not prove any model candidate's performance, a customer improvement, human setup-time savings, customer deployment or production reliability. A fresh support model campaign still needs a separate safe campaign plan and explicit spend approval.
