## Integrity and safety hardening

The checkpoint was delayed after a skeptical audit found real weaknesses. Those weaknesses were fixed before evidence was sealed:

- an upstream review or structural recompilation now invalidates all downstream candidate, qualification and local-acceptance artifacts;
- the complete proposal → source → confirmation → work plan → structural binding → action/observer → qualification chain revalidates after restart;
- all required systems, not merely one system, must reach a local stage before the aggregate journey shows that stage;
- action operations must cover the structurally compiled operation set exactly once and cannot widen authority;
- action and observer runtimes must match exact binding, surface, implementation, source, runtime-schema, transport and reviewed credential-alias identities;
- a synthetic disposable-local challenge verifies distinct action and observer principals, and the observer principal must prove read-only scope;
- observer calls are checked for zero observer writes and zero state mutation;
- observer evidence has a strict allowlisted structure, rejects response-derived fields at any depth, rejects future-dated evidence, and refuses a pre-existing matching result;
- missing world write/state measurements fail closed instead of defaulting to zero;
- lost-response recovery must cross a fresh process boundary while preserving the same durable-store identity;
- proof rules remain explicitly engineer-owned, reviewed and unproved, and are bound to the exact role-owner confirmation and success-criteria digest.

## Exact boundary

It does not establish arbitrary API support, customer-environment acceptance, customer credential handling, customer runtime authority, executable comparison readiness, production security, production reliability, human setup-time savings, customer use or activation.

The synthetic local authentication challenge establishes that two distinct reviewed local principals and a read-only observer scope were exercised. It does not establish a customer's real identity provider, credential lifecycle or production authentication.

The action runtime, observer runtime and fictional world were still engineer-authored for this fixture. DAS generated and integrity-bound the preparation and qualification chain, but did not autonomously invent customer-specific idempotency, reconciliation, outcome or proof semantics.
