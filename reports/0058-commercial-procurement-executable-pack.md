# Commercial procurement executable pack

Date: 2026-08-05

## Result

The commercial Level 1 comparison boundary now has its first executable role pack. A completed procurement onboarding record is joined to a disposable multi-warehouse company, a namespaced system driver, an independent external-state verifier, an imported current-agent package, three mandatory manual/general baselines and four compiler candidates.

The comparison contract freezes:

- five development cases;
- two validation cases;
- three adversarial cases;
- two sealed prospective unseen cases;
- exact participant configuration hashes;
- the system-driver and verifier binding;
- zero unsafe and zero incorrect-side-effect gates;
- three fresh repeat runs;
- a ten-percent cost-reduction target;
- a ten-percent speed-reduction target; and
- a hard campaign budget and wall-clock limit.

The runner no longer treats quality as the only possible improvement promise. A replacement may prove an equal-quality upgrade through separately frozen cost and speed reductions. Every declared target must pass; otherwise the imported current agent is retained.

## Deterministic preflight

The outcome-aware reference passed all 12 cases:

- development: 5/5;
- validation: 2/2;
- adversarial: 3/3;
- unseen: 2/2.

Three misleading shortcuts failed:

- do nothing: 3/12;
- order every demand: 4/12;
- choose the cheapest offer: 6/12.

This establishes that the pack is executable and discriminating before any paid model campaign. The namespaced driver prevents a candidate from silently reaching another system. The verifier distinguishes missing work from an incorrect side effect and remains outside the candidate.

## Exact boundary

This is a zero-cost disposable-world preflight. It proves that one commercial role contract is executable, independently verifiable and suitable for a frozen comparison. It does not prove that any model-backed compiler candidate wins, that a real company saves time, that the import format covers arbitrary external agents, or that the system is production-ready.

The next dependency is the joined model-backed comparison under a separately approved spend cap, followed by recommendation, evidence review and controlled activation/export.
