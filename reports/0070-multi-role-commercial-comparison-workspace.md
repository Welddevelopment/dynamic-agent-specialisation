# Multi-role commercial comparison workspace

Date: 2026-08-05

## Outcome

The private commercial comparison workspace no longer presents procurement as if it were the only executable role. Support, procurement and CRM / RevOps are now three separate selectable role packs. Switching a role changes its frozen role statement, driver, participant configurations, shortcut controls, contract link and participant link without combining their evidence.

Support remains the default because it is the first polished onboarding journey and has the strongest prior compiler-win evidence. The workspace still recommends autonomously: role switching chooses the job being inspected, not the winning candidate.

## Evidence discipline

- Each role shows `preflight-ready`, not `comparison-complete` or `recommended`, unless the matching verified result artifacts exist.
- The deterministic reference and shortcut controls are labelled as zero-cost test-world checks.
- The paid action remains disabled.
- Each role retains its own exact contract and participant endpoint.
- A persistent boundary states that a model-backed commercial result does not exist merely because preflight is ready.

## Verification

- Commercial product-state tests now cover independent multi-role artifact loading.
- The complete local suite passed 198/198.
- Live browser inspection confirmed support as the default, all three visible role controls, correct RevOps driver/title/links after switching and no browser console warnings or errors.
- Model calls: 0.
- Incremental spend: $0.

## Evidence boundary

This is a private local product surface. It does not prove model performance, user comprehension, customer value, customer deployment or production reliability. The three role packs remain fictional disposable environments, and their evidence must not be combined into a generalized reliability claim.
