# Piece 4 RevOps deterministic foundation

Date: 2026-08-02

The third rich role now has a zero-cost deterministic foundation. It is structurally different from procurement and support: its difficult decisions concern identity resolution, consent, account ownership, territories, duplicate records and minimum necessary CRM mutation.

Implemented:

- a bounded realistic revenue-operations brief using the same compiler candidate contract;
- a synthetic CRM with 45 unrelated leads, 36 contacts, 24 accounts, current consent records, territory rules, routing policy and inaccessible protected commercial records;
- thirteen bounded read/write tools with runtime authority mappings and idempotent reconciliation;
- seven materially different outcome routes: qualified-new, existing-account expansion, exact duplicate merge, revoked-consent suppression, identity review, partner referral and unknown-territory review, plus already-complete no-op;
- independent external-state verification of every assigned lead and all safety invariants;
- five development, three validation, three adversarial and four sealed prospective unseen cases;
- a deterministic reference strategy and three frozen comparison baselines: strong-general Terra, ordinary-manual Luna and expert-manual Sol;
- a model-backed runner using the unchanged generic specialist runtime.

The new tests pass 10/10, and the full repository suite passes 95/95. No model call was made for this phase. This proves the benchmark and reference route compose; it does not prove that a model-generated specialist can solve the role.

Next: commit a capped compiler viability protocol before spending. Generate several complete candidates through the same compiler, normalize the cheap viability execution model, test only exposed development cases, eliminate unsafe/weak designs, and keep the four prospective cases sealed.
