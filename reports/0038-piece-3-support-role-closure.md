# Piece 3 support role closure

Date: 2026-08-02

## Result

The realistic SaaS-support role is now closed as the second technically complete rich role for the bounded Level 1 programme. The final compiler-produced specialist passed the frozen Cycle 4 comparison, ranked first under the precommitted safety/outcome/cost ordering, and then passed the final repeatability gate 12/12 with zero unsafe attempts.

This closes one role. It does not complete bounded Level 1, prove universal reliability, prove customer value, or prove that the compiler reduces prospective human setup effort. A third substantially different rich role is still required.

## Preserved chronology

The result was not clean on the first attempt, and those failures remain part of the evidence:

1. The earlier support specialist missed one original unseen case. It completed 7/8 safely but repeated knowledge-base search instead of exhausting that route and escalating a novel defect to product engineering.
2. A general evidence-exhaustion repair produced a later perfect unseen result, but its first repeatability campaign completed only 17/18. On one duplicate-charge case it applied the correct credit and sent the correct response, but failed to record the required closure and added an unnecessary escalation.
3. The next repair introduced a general route-locking rule: after evidence selects a route, complete every required outcome for that route before considering another route or declaring success. The duplicate-charge example was named because it was a demonstrated failure, but the rule applies across routes.
4. The repaired candidate passed 44/44 exposed regression cases, then 3/3 fresh validation and 3/3 adversarial cases.
5. On four subsequently released prospective cases, it passed 4/4 with perfect externally verified outcomes and zero unsafe attempts. The strong general Terra baseline and expert Sol baseline also passed 4/4 safely; the compiler specialist ranked first because it cost `$0.0475268`, versus `$0.334318` and `$0.84549`. The ordinary Luna baseline passed 3/4 safely at `$0.0395414`.
6. The final candidate then ran three fresh repeats of all four prospective cases. It passed 12/12, with zero unsafe attempts, three correct handoffs, 170 tool calls and `$0.13782784` spend.

## Evidence controls

- The candidate, role brief, runtime contracts, baselines and prospective-case digest were frozen before the Cycle 4 cases were released.
- Every business outcome was scored from synthetic external system state by an independent verifier. The candidate did not grade itself.
- Each repeat used a fresh model gateway and empty response cache.
- Safety and authority remained hard gates.
- Failed earlier campaigns were preserved rather than overwritten.
- The final evidence ledger verified successfully.
