# Sealed post-comparison lifecycle handoff

Date: 2026-08-05

## Result

The final structural break between a fresh commercial model comparison and the existing Level 1.5 replacement controller is now closed.

Each of the three commercial roles has a second, separately committed two-case offline gate. These cases are not part of candidate construction, development, validation, adversarial testing, unseen selection, or repeatability. The paid campaign plan contains neither their payloads nor their identifiers. A gate can release only after receiving the exact integrity-checked lifecycle plan, exact campaign result, exact selected specialist bundle, and an exact proved compiler-winner handoff.

The lifecycle handoff plan binds:

- the active bundle, activation, candidate and no-spend re-comparison request;
- the frozen comparison contract;
- the exact role-specific model campaign id, plan hash and pricing-table hash; and
- the sealed post-comparison gate hash.

The result handoff accepts a challenger only when the exact campaign completed with a valid evidence ledger, selected a different compiler candidate, and proved every frozen improvement target. A retained current agent produces a `retain-current-specialist` record and cannot open the vault. The challenger record grants no model-spend, activation or customer-write authority.

## Fresh gate worlds

- Procurement: transfer plus approved residual purchase; confirmed inbound plus an exact over-authority remainder.
- Support: four-route mixed queue; safe product work before a precise financial-approval handoff.
- RevOps: consent precedence across customer/partner/new routes; exact duplicate, identity conflict and lost-response reconciliation in one isolated batch.

The deterministic role references passed 6/6 total fresh cases. The do-nothing controls passed 0/6. The vaults remained unreleased during preflight.

## Validation

- Full local suite: 214/214 passing.
- New focused tests: 4/4 passing.
- Model calls: 0.
- New paid spend: $0.
- Gate contracts and preflight receipt: `artifacts/commercial/postcomparison-gates-v1/`.

Mutation of the handoff candidate fails integrity validation. A self-consistent handoff alone is insufficient: release rechecks the complete plan → campaign result → bundle → candidate chain. A current-agent retention result cannot release cases. Campaign-plan serialization was checked to contain no post-comparison case identifier or payload digest.

## Evidence boundary

This proves a zero-cost structural evidence chain and executable fresh disposable gates. It does not prove that a newly generated model specialist improves on the active agent. The preflight used deterministic reference and weak-control strategies only. Full empirical bounded Level 1.5 still requires separate explicit approval for a fresh paid, drift-triggered role campaign, followed by the exact winner passing these offline cases, zero-authority shadow, explicitly authorized canary, and promotion gates. Customer value and production reliability remain unproved.
