# Piece 3 baseline and measurement checkpoint

Three serious support-role baselines are now defined before paid role testing:

1. a minimally configured strong general Terra agent;
2. an ordinary manually configured Luna support specialist; and
3. a carefully engineered Sol specialist with explicit exposed-world controls.

All receive the same bounded tools, company evidence, authority ceiling and independent verifier as compiler candidates. Their fingerprints are distinct.

The support runner records strict pass/fail, graded external outcome score, unsafe attempts, correct handoffs, model cost, elapsed time and tool calls.

Human-effort protocols are also frozen prospectively. No human setup session has yet been observed, so no setup-time advantage may be claimed. This prevents an estimated three-hour or sixteen-hour budget from being presented as measured labour.

This checkpoint establishes fair comparison boundaries. It contains no paid support-role result.
