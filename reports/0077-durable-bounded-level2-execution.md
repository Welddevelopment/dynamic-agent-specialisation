# Durable bounded Level 2 execution

Date: 2026-08-05

## Result

The bounded fleet plan is now joined to a durable, explicit execution-control boundary.

An accountable owner must authorize the exact plan hash, exact assignment-hash set and an actual-cost ceiling no broader than the company contract. Each assignment outcome must then bind to the exact workload, specialist fingerprint and verifier selected during planning. Duplicate observations are idempotent; a conflicting duplicate fails. State persists with an integrity hash and reloads without losing completion or gap status.

One failed verification, incomplete assignment, unsafe attempt, incorrect side effect or aggregate cost breach halts the fleet and keeps the parent goal incomplete. The controller reports parent completion only when every assignment passes and no role gap remains. An approved role gap can prepare a separate Level 1 contract request, but grants zero model-spend, role-creation or activation authority.

## Joined fictional rehearsal

The previously selected plan produced four bounded assignment batches:

- 30 support tickets to the first support specialist;
- 30 support tickets to the second support specialist;
- 20 approved procurement demands to the procurement specialist; and
- 25 CRM leads to the RevOps specialist.

All 105 individual fictional items ran in fresh disposable role worlds through deterministic role-reference strategies. Each complete batch passed its own existing independent external-state verifier. The controller then persisted and reloaded `routable-work-completed-role-gap-blocked`; the broad parent goal remained incomplete because ten finance items still lacked a proved specialist. The exact finance role gap was prepared for Level 1 with zero authorities granted.

## Failure controls

- Wrong plan hash or incomplete assignment approval is rejected.
- An unsafe independently observed assignment halts all further new outcomes.
- Conflicting duplicate results are rejected while exact duplicates remain idempotent after terminal state.
- Mutated durable state fails integrity validation on reload.
- Parent completion is impossible while a role gap exists.

## Evidence boundary

The executed specialists were deterministic reference strategies, not newly model-compiled agents. The worlds, workload and owner were fictional and local. This proves joined planning, authority, dispatch-evidence, independent verification, durable aggregation, halt and role-gap mechanics—not model-agent performance, real company strategy, customer value, production scheduling or full Level 2 completion.

The largest remaining Level 2 mechanism gap is the role-gap return loop: turn the approved finance gap into a fully separate Level 1 contract, prove a specialist, pass Level 1.5 activation, add it to the portfolio, replan only the blocked residual and complete the original broad goal without repeating the already verified 105 items.
