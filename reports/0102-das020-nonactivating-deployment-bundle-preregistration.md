# DAS-020 nonactivating deployment-bundle preregistration

Date frozen: 2026-08-14  
Work item: DAS-020 / zero-spend productization lane  
State at freeze: no DAS-020 implementation or rehearsal counted as evidence

## Question

Can DAS package one selected or clearly provisional specialist, its exact role and source chain, the DAS-025 plugin-project identities, separately supplied implementation identities, local conformance evidence, independent-observer declarations, rollback plan, restart-safe state, readiness receipt and every unresolved blocker into one signed customer-local bundle without turning any upstream fictional or non-executable evidence into customer execution readiness?

## Frozen boundary

The first rehearsal will use a clearly labelled synthetic/provisional specialist. It is not an eligible customer specialist and cannot be activated. The package may preserve exact fictional disposable conformance evidence, but must keep all of the following false:

- customer execution readiness;
- executable customer operations;
- runtime or model-spend authority;
- customer-environment conformance;
- mandatory customer acceptance;
- comparison completion;
- controlled activation; and
- customer or production evidence.

The package must not contain credential values. Credential aliases may be recorded only as customer-local references.

## Frozen package contract

One bundle must include integrity-bound representations of:

1. tenant, role, revision and source identities;
2. one synthetic/provisional or separately proved selected specialist;
3. DAS-025 generated action/observer project identities, still non-executable;
4. separate action and observer implementation-content identities;
5. exact conformance evidence and its fictional/local scope;
6. separate action and independently authenticated read-only observer bindings;
7. a nonactivating rollback plan whose safe fallback leaves or restores the prior specialist state;
8. an integrity-bound readiness receipt;
9. a generated exact blocker ledger;
10. restart-safe, owner-only runtime state; and
11. an Ed25519-signed exact file manifest verified against an externally pinned public key.

The signing private key must never enter the package. The package must reject overwrite and unexpected files.

## Mandatory blockers for the provisional rehearsal

DAS, rather than the caller, must derive and preserve at least:

- selected-specialist-not-proved;
- customer-credentials-not-resolved;
- customer-action-transport-not-bound;
- customer-observer-transport-not-bound;
- customer-environment-conformance-not-run;
- mandatory-customer-acceptance-not-run;
- comparison-readiness-unproved;
- customer-execution-not-authorized; and
- activation-not-authorized.

The bundle must remain blocked if a caller omits or contradicts any of these.

## Restart and rollback contract

The durable state may transition only through nonactivating preparation states such as `packaged-nonactivating`, `preview-loaded-nonactivating` and `rolled-back-nonactivating`. Every revision must bind the prior state hash, bundle hash, specialist identity and blocker hash. Restart must preserve the exact revision and refuse mutation.

Rollback may restore the recorded prior-package pointer or the explicit `leave-current-specialist-unchanged` fallback. It must not grant activation, execution, write, credential or spend authority.

## Precommitted attacks

The implementation and sealed rehearsal must reject or detect:

1. tenant, role, role revision or source mutation;
2. stale conformance evidence;
3. cross-specialist, cross-role or cross-package evidence;
4. changed plugin-project or implementation identities;
5. action/observer source or authentication collapse;
6. observer write permission or action-response-as-proof;
7. omitted mandatory blocker;
8. caller-supplied readiness or authority widening;
9. credential or secret leakage;
10. unsigned, wrongly signed, self-signed-with-a-different-key or post-signature-mutated artifacts;
11. unexpected files, permissive sensitive-file modes or overwrite;
12. mutated restart state, stale revision and cross-bundle rollback; and
13. any attempt to transition the package to active or executable.

## Measures

Record:

- files packaged and exact signed manifest coverage;
- source, role, specialist, plugin-project, implementation, conformance, observer, readiness, blocker and rollback identities;
- mandatory blockers derived/preserved;
- restart revision and state integrity;
- rollback result;
- attacks passed/required;
- executable customer operations;
- every authority/readiness gate;
- model calls, spend and active machine time, explicitly not human setup time.

## Pass rule

DAS-020 passes only if the signed package is complete and reloadable, every identity is bound, the exact blocker ledger survives restart, rollback is safe and durable, every precommitted attack fails closed, and all execution/activation gates remain false. A package that is structurally complete but unsigned, stale, missing blockers or self-asserting customer readiness fails.

## Stop rules

- Preserve failed or superseded rehearsals.
- Do not weaken an upstream identity or evidence check to make packaging succeed.
- Do not generate an activation receipt.
- Do not call a model, contact a network service or spend money.
- Do not call synthetic/local conformance customer conformance.
- Do not claim human setup time, customer deployment or production readiness.

## Claim boundary

A pass would establish only that DAS can assemble and integrity-protect the exact artifacts required to hand a still-blocked specialist package to a customer-local environment, preserve why it is blocked, and reload or roll it back safely. It would not establish a selected customer specialist, working customer bindings, customer acceptance, activation, deployment, customer value or production reliability.
