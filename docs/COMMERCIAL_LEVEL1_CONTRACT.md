# Commercial Level 1 product contract

## Promise

A non-specialist company user can describe a supported business role, supply policies and representative work, set authority and operating priorities, optionally import an existing agent, and receive an automatically recommended specialist with a plain-English comparison and an inspectable technical evidence trail.

The product recommends autonomously. Human review is available at executive, engineering and forensic depth, but routine use must not require the customer to design prompts, select models or assemble an agent framework.

## Honest readiness stages

### 1. Design preview

The product has enough information to draft the role, identify missing decisions and propose a candidate/search plan. This is not performance evidence.

### 2. Ready for comparison

The role boundary, representative cases, policies, authority, current-agent decision, priorities and independent success checks are sufficiently defined to run a fair comparison. This stage means the contract is ready; it does not mean a comparison has run or an improvement has been proved. A later comparison result is still local or sandbox evidence unless explicitly stated otherwise.

### 3. Controlled activation

Every required system operation has an executable customer-local adapter, credentials are configured outside the product record, independent outcome verification is executable, a safe sandbox or approved bounded environment exists, and all activation gates pass. Generated descriptions or declared tests never count as executable evidence.

## Initial supported role families

1. SaaS support operations — first polished commercial path; strongest existing compiler-win evidence and a complete executable disposable commercial comparison pack.
2. Procurement coverage — complete executable disposable commercial pack; prior evidence retained an existing specialist.
3. CRM / revenue operations — complete executable disposable commercial pack; prior evidence retained an existing specialist.

The shared commercial engine works across all three. Support retains the first complete onboarding and demonstration journey. Unsupported roles may receive a draft preview, but cannot be represented as comparison- or activation-ready without a new role adapter and verifier.

Procurement, support and CRM / revenue operations now all have complete executable disposable commercial packs. This does not mean all three have fresh model-backed comparison results: support and RevOps still require independently approved paid campaigns, and historical procurement evidence retained the existing specialist rather than proving an upgrade.

All three also have separate zero-cost frozen model-campaign plans on one resumable campaign runner. Each role has its own campaign identity, approval phrase and durable state. Approval for one role cannot authorize another; no paid campaign is enabled merely because its plan exists.

The private comparison workspace exposes the three executable packs separately. Each selection loads only that role's frozen contract, participants, deterministic controls and evidence boundary. Support is the default polished journey; changing roles never counts as choosing a candidate or proving an upgrade.

All three initial roles have now crossed the shared packaged customer-local activation path in disposable local rehearsals. Support and RevOps each passed 17/17 package gates, authenticated loopback execution, independent outcome verification, durable status, monitoring and duplicate suppression. These used deterministic action paths and do not substitute for a fresh model comparison or customer deployment.

A customer binding kit now scaffolds the remaining company-specific system boundary without treating generated files as implementation. It requires exact operation coverage, authority mapping, write idempotency/reconciliation, secret references, an independent verifier and ten safe acceptance artifacts before controlled activation. See `docs/COMMERCIAL_CUSTOMER_BINDING_KIT.md`.

## Customer journey

1. Describe the company and desired role in ordinary language.
2. Choose a supported role family or request an unsupported-role preview.
3. Map systems, knowledge sources and possible actions.
4. Confirm policies, forbidden actions, approvals and escalation ownership.
5. Add representative historical or synthetic cases.
6. Define externally observable success.
7. Set quality, cost, speed and intervention priorities.
8. Import the current agent, or state that no current agent exists.
9. Review the generated role contract and answer only consequential missing questions.
10. Run a bounded comparison.
11. Receive the recommended specialist, alternatives, evidence, cost and decision rationale.
12. Activate only after the separate controlled-activation gates pass.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No credentials are stored in onboarding records.
- A candidate cannot grade itself.
- Safety and authority remain hard gates.
- Existing agents are retained when no upgrade is proved.
- Unsupported role previews are labelled clearly.
- Generated cases and verifier descriptions do not count as passed tests.
- No customer, production, human-effort or framework-superiority claim is inferred from local fictional evidence.
