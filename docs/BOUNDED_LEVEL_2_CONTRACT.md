# Bounded Level 2 contract

Level 1 builds and proves one specialist. Level 1.5 monitors and safely improves that specialist. Bounded Level 2 coordinates a portfolio of already-proved specialists against a trusted mixed workload and identifies when a genuinely new role is required.

## Current technical finish line

1. Accept one ordinary broad company goal plus workload inventory from trusted bounded adapters.
2. Require each workload class to declare its externally observable outcome, volume, source, deadline, quality floor, unit-cost ceiling, risk, systems, tools, context, authority, policy and independent verifier.
3. Admit only integrity-checked active specialists with perfect frozen pass evidence, zero unsafe attempts, explicit capacity, cost and latency measurements, and an exact capability boundary.
4. Compare quality-first, cost-first, speed-first and company-weighted allocation plans.
5. Route only to specialists whose systems, tools, context, authority, policy, verifier, quality, cost and latency all fit the work.
6. Split a workload across multiple compatible specialists when one specialist lacks capacity.
7. Keep the hard company cost limit and every specialist capacity limit.
8. Leave unsupported or capacity-exhausted work unassigned. Group genuinely identical gaps into the smallest role proposal.
9. A role proposal must begin with zero model-spend, creation and activation authority and require explicit human approval plus a separate Level 1 comparison.
10. Independently verify the final plan's identities, compatibility, exact coverage/gaps, capacity, cost, proposal count and lack of automatic authority.
11. Preserve alternatives and exact reasons while recommending one plan automatically.
12. Separate planning, execution authorization, role creation and activation. Passing one cannot silently grant the next.

## What this is not

- arbitrary strategic decomposition from unconstrained company data;
- proof that the system understands which departments a real company should have;
- a license to create agents, spend money or activate workers automatically;
- a replacement for independent specialist-level outcome verification;
- a production scheduler, distributed queue or customer deployment;
- the complete fleet-brain vision.

## Status — 2026-08-05

The deterministic planning foundation is implemented. In a fictional four-stream company goal it allocated 105 of 115 workload units across four proved specialist records, split one workload across two support specialists because of capacity, stayed at an estimated $7.25 under a $10 hard ceiling, and isolated the remaining finance work into one zero-authority role proposal. The independent verifier passed the plan.

Durable explicit execution control is also implemented. An owner must authorize the exact plan, assignment set and cost ceiling. In the joined local rehearsal, deterministic specialist references processed all 105 routed fictional items across four batches in the real disposable support, procurement and RevOps worlds; every batch passed its existing role-specific external-state verifier. State survived reload and the parent correctly remained blocked on the unresolved finance role. An unsafe assignment halts the fleet, exact duplicates are idempotent and state mutation fails closed.

The bounded role-gap return loop is now joined deterministically. The separately approved finance requirement entered the existing generic Level 1 compiler, which compared multiple candidates and selected `finance-close:balanced` with a 100% pass rate and zero safety violations in its frozen fictional cases. The exact selected specialist was registered and activated under the finance policy, authority and independent-verifier boundary, then added to the fleet portfolio.

The expanded plan preserved the hashes of all four already completed assignments and routed only the ten previously blocked finance items. Those ten items ran through the disposable finance world and passed 10/10 external-state checks with zero safety violations. The controller carried the prior 105 verified items without rerunning them, survived reload, and changed the parent state to `original-broad-goal-completed` only after all 115/115 items were covered and independently verified.

This closes the bounded Level 2 **mechanism** in one deterministic fictional four-stream environment: broad-goal allocation, specialist matching, safe capacity split, honest role-gap detection, separate Level 1 specialist construction/selection, bounded activation, residual-only replan, external verification and original-goal completion. It does not establish model-backed specialist quality, arbitrary role discovery, general company understanding, customer value, production reliability or a commercially complete fleet brain. The empirical Level 1.5 gate also remains separate and still requires a newly generated model-backed challenger plus explicit paid approval.

The same planner and independent verifier now also pass five zero-cost fictional operating profiles: an expanded standard day, a support-heavy surge, a compressed deadline with two honest gaps, a role-proposal-limit refusal and a hard-budget refusal. Correct refusal is a separately verified outcome: every preserved alternative must violate a declared hard bound, the blocker label must be exact, specialist identities must remain intact and no authority may be granted. These additional profiles strengthen structural planning generality; they do not add model-backed or customer execution evidence.

Actual Level 1 selections can now enter fleet planning through an evidence-preserving admission bridge. The bridge reloads the durable registry, checks the candidate and selection chain, verifies referenced source artifacts, prevents any tool/context/authority widening and derives conservative planning bounds without presenting owner-set capacity as throughput proof. The existing procurement, support and RevOps selections formed a 3/3 independently verified planning route with zero authority granted. This joins real historical Level 1 selection evidence to fleet planning, not to fleet execution or customer traffic.

The same three admitted selections can also import their preserved model-backed current-runtime receipts through the durable fleet controller. Exact candidate fingerprints, independent checks and historical cost are preserved; three receipts completed the three-item parent state, survived reload and were idempotent on duplicate import. Those source runs predate the fleet plan, so this proves historical receipt compatibility and aggregation—not a prospective Level 2 model campaign.

Trusted customer-local Fleet Intake is now joined to planning. A versioned adapter descriptor pins an exact tenant, source system, bounded operation, outcome and complete specialist requirement. A fresh integrity-checked workload snapshot may select only those pre-verified operations; it cannot invent a requirement or grant execution, model-spend, role-creation or activation authority. Intake then translates the adapter-declared work into the existing bounded fleet contract before the independent planner and verifier run.

In the fictional joined fixture, fresh procurement, support and RevOps snapshots compiled into an exact three-item contract and the already-admitted Level 1 selections formed a 3/3 verified plan. Stale, future-dated, cross-tenant, mutated and unsupported-operation inputs fail closed. This removes hand-authored internal workload contracts from the normal bounded path. It does not autonomously interpret arbitrary company state or strategically decompose an unconstrained goal: the ordinary goal is human supplied and workload classification remains the responsibility of trusted customer-local adapters.
