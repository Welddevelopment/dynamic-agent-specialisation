## Operating policy

- Keep at most one primary implementation item and one noncontending audit/maintenance item active.
- Maintain two lanes when useful work exists:
  - **Lane A — time to safe first specialist:** remove author-only setup knowledge and reduce customer/engineer steps without inventing authority or evidence.
  - **Lane B — autonomous safe specialist quality:** improve candidate design, fair evaluation and lifecycle decisions against strong adaptive baselines.
- Deterministic work comes first. A paid gate is allowed only after frozen inputs, safety review, scoring, durable budget enforcement and targeted tests pass.
- Default paid limit: at most **$1 per gate** and **$3 cumulative before a new checkpoint**. One paid campaign at a time. Never retry an ambiguous outcome automatically.
- Kill an item after two coherent checkpoints if it has not reduced a blocker, produced a reusable primitive or changed a technical decision.
- Preserve failures, costs, private evidence and the separation from Capability Factory.
