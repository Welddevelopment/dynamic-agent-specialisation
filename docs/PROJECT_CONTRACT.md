# Bounded Level 1 contract

## Required input

1. Outcome
2. Environment and available systems
3. Policies
4. Authority
5. Examples
6. Success criteria
7. Operating priorities such as quality, cost, and latency

## Complete candidate

A candidate is not a prompt. It contains a model policy, instructions, context policy, tools, memory, authority, escalation, verifier binding, cost/latency limits, provenance, and version.

## Complete bounded Level 1 finish line

- One compiler core across at least three substantially different business roles.
- Multiple meaningfully different complete candidates per role.
- Strong general, ordinary-manual, and expert-manual baselines.
- Frozen unseen cases with leakage controls.
- Independent external-state verification.
- Measurement of success, side effects, authority violations, escalation, human configuration work, cost, latency, repeatability, and optimisation gain.
- Failures and exact paid costs preserved.
- Winner exported as a versioned reusable specialist; strong alternatives preserved.

## Evidence boundary

Deterministic synthetic evaluation proves evaluation machinery, not model quality, customer usefulness, production reliability, or market demand. Paid model evidence begins only after separate approval.
